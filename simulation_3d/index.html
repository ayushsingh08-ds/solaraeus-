Institutional Review & Model Boundary Limitations
PROVISIONAL_TERRAIN_TREE_RESULTS

Tree dimensions and canopy extinction are based on photographic estimates and nominal municipal census data. Results represent provisional microclimate sensitivities, not certified field-calibrated microclimates.

TREE_GEOMETRY_PHOTO_ESTIMATED_ONLY

Crown diameter, crown base height, and trunk dimensions have been derived from calibrated street-level imagery and BBMP census attributes; unverified field lidar is unavailable.

SYNTHETIC_TERRAIN_ONLY

The ground elevation model utilizes validated synthetic profiles and FABDEM regional reference datasets. Measured street-scale DTM is deferred to future municipal surveys.

CLOUD_MODEL_PARAMETRIC_NOT_OBSERVED

In the absence of synchronous local radiometric cloud ceilometer observations, cloud attenuation is driven by a physically-coupled fractal parametric model.

VISUAL_STYLE_PRESENTATION_LAYER_ONLY

All aesthetic treatments (atmospheric haze, corner mist, twilight lighting) reside strictly in the presentation layer and are mathematically isolated from the radiative solver.

VISUAL_ONLY_ELEMENTS_EXCLUDED_FROM_PHYSICS

Decorative perimeter vegetation, vignette filters, and UI indicators do not cast physical ray-traced shadows and do not modify SVF or mean radiant temperature.
